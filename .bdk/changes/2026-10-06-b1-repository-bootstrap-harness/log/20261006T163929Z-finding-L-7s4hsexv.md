Issue #1 goal: the harness "records cost, turns and wall time". A discarded run gets `metrics: {}` (`hook.ts` afterRun), so its turns and wall time are lost. design.md:68 leaves them to the smoke suite's `measure`, so B3 must repeat them. Decide whether the harness writes turns and wall time into the row itself (one place, also for discarded runs).
