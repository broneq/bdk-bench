architecture.md:70 says working copies, config homes "and the fixture cache" live in `${XDG_CACHE_HOME:-~/.cache}/bdk-bench/<checkout>/<suite>/<series>/`. In T43 the fixture base is cached at `join(RUNS_DIR, "cache")` (`suites/regression/suite.ts:197`), i.e. `evals/.runs/cache`, and the series sandbox is wiped at the start of every series (`rmSync(sandbox, ...)`, `suite.ts:192`). A cache inside the per-series sandbox would re-fetch the fixture and re-run `npm ci` on every series, contradicting design.md:61 ("cached, `npm ci` once into the base"). Name the cache location explicitly (shared, outside the per-series sandbox) in both files.
