06-smoke-suite-and-entry.md:50: `smokeRunner(io, deps)` takes `{ assertCommitted, prepareFixture, evaluate }`, and the test calls `run({ probe: true, ... })`. Every other location is the real one: `rmSync` and render into `<ROOT_DIR>/.runs/series/smoke/<series>`, `rmSync` of the real sandbox under `${XDG_CACHE_HOME:-~/.cache}/bdk-bench/`, `readRows` of `results/smoke/<series>.jsonl`, the real `LEDGER_FILE` for the summary, and `readVersions()` of the real file. A unit test that leaves directories in the repository and the user's cache is not hermetic. Inject the locations (runs dir, sandbox root, results root, ledger) or the series-directory factory too, and state what `run` returns when `assertCommitted` throws ("returns its refusal": a rejected promise, or a printed message and an exit code?).
