Problem: the report formatters are exported, but stats.test.ts covers only median, range and compare. Two median tests also overlap.

Why it matters: the output format is a public contract (BDK-CQ-7, BDK-TQ-9); the "n/a", "fewer than 2 counted runs" and "[low..high]" branches can regress unseen.

Suggested fix: add cases for formatValues([]) = "n/a", formatValues([1,2,4]) = "2 [1..4]", formatVerdict with 1 run and with a clear gap; merge the duplicate median test.
