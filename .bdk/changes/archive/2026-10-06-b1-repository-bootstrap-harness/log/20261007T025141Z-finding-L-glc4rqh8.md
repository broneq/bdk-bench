Problem: Severity low, not blocking. The range adds three exported entry points to harness/paths.ts: `seriesDir(runsDir, suite, series)`, `seriesNames(resultsDir, suite)`, and a second parameter `fallbackHome` on `cacheHome`, and changes the documented fallback of `SANDBOX_DIR`/`PROMPTFOO_CONFIG_DIR` from `~/.cache` (meaning `$HOME`) to the password-database home. Plan 03-2 lists the module's exports and does not name any of them; plan 06 still states main.ts's `rawDir` as a literal `<RUNS_DIR>/series/<suite>/<series>/raw`; plan 03-4 and design.md/architecture.md still describe the cache as `${XDG_CACHE_HOME:-~/.cache}`. This is code with no stated behaviour, a sibling of the deferred L-x53cb90l (resultsFile signature drift).

Why it matters: These are the harness's shared path contract (the `public-api` risk area: exported functions other modules and future suites consume). Each later suite (B2+) will take the plan as its reference and re-derive paths the plan still spells literally, recreating the duplication the fix removed.

Suggested fix: Amend plan 03-2 (exports and test cases), 03-4 (fallback test case) and 06 (main.ts `rawDir` via `seriesDir`), and the cache-directory sentence in design.md and architecture.md, to the fallback actually implemented; resolve together with L-x53cb90l.
